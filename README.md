# Month-End Close & Balance Sheet Reconciliation

> **Portfolio case study | Fictional business data | Senior Finance & Accounting**

## Business context

**Business:** Northstar Cloud Services Inc.  
**Role demonstrated:** Senior Accountant / Month-End Close

A practical month-end close simulation covering trial balance review, reconciliations, accruals, prepayments and close controls.

## Business problem

The objective was to take accounting data and turn it into a controlled, reviewable finance process with clear supporting schedules and exceptions.

## Approach

1. Reviewed source accounting data and supporting schedules.
2. Reconciled relevant balances and investigated differences.
3. Separated timing differences from accounting exceptions.
4. Prepared supporting schedules and proposed accounting actions.
5. Produced a management-ready output.
6. Documented assumptions and unresolved items rather than forcing unexplained balances to zero.

## Key findings

- AR subledger agrees to the GL with no variance.
- Four month-end accruals were identified for review/posting.
- Prepaid insurance and software require monthly amortization.
- Bank reconciliation contains timing and posting items requiring follow-up.
- Deferred revenue is tracked separately from recognized revenue.
